## Understanding cognitive control, automated word reading, and how response latency reflects mental conflict resolution.

*Published: 2026-11-01*

### The Mechanism of Cognitive Conflict

First described by John Ridley Stroop in 1935, the Stroop effect illustrates the interference between automated cognitive routines and intentional task demands.

When naming the font color of an incongruent word (e.g., the word 'RED' printed in blue ink), reaction times are systematically slower compared to congruent stimuli.

This difference, termed conflict cost or interference latency, reflects the executive time required by the anterior cingulate cortex and prefrontal regions to suppress automatic reading.

### Deriving Conflict and Interference Costs

In modern cognitive benchmarking, two distinct metrics are derived: congruent response latency and incongruent response latency.

The net difference represents interference cost. Lower conflict costs indicate superior selective attention and inhibitory control.
